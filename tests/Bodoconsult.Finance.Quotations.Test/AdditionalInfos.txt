NASDAQ Eurozone Real Estate Index (NQEURO8600) 

The NASDAQ Eurozone Real Estate Index is a float adjusted market capitalization-weighted index designed to track the performance of securities in NASDAQ Eurozone Index that are classified in the Real Estate Supersector according to Industry Classification Benchmark (ICB) assigned to the Eurozone. The Index began on December 3, 2012 at a base value of 1000. Components: 53 Currency: USD Entitlements: NASDAQ Global Developed Europe Total Market Value: is the sum of the market value of all companies in the index. Dividend Market Value: is the sum of the market value of all dividends generated by companies in the index.
